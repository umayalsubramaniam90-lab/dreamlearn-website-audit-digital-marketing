## About This Project

This capstone project delivers a full audit of the DreamLearn e-learning platform and a complete digital marketing strategy to grow its visibility and conversions — covering on-page, technical, and off-page SEO; a prioritized website redesign plan; an SEO and content marketing strategy; a paid advertising and social media plan; and live implementation of analytics tracking and key audit fixes.

## Deliverables

| Document | Description |
|---|---|
| [**Website Redesign Proposal**](./DreamLearn_Website_Redesign_Proposal.pdf) | Consolidated audit (content/SEO, technical, off-page, search visibility), page-by-page findings, and a prioritized redesign action plan. |
| [**Digital Marketing Strategy**](./DreamLearn_Digital_Marketing_Strategy.pdf) | SEO strategy with keyword-to-page mapping, an 8-week content marketing calendar, a paid advertising plan (Google & Meta), and a social media promotion plan. |
| [**Summary Presentation**](./DreamLearn_Summary_Presentation.pdf) | A visual summary of the audit findings, strategy, and implementation evidence. |
